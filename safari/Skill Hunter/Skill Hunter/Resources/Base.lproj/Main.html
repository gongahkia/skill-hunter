Safari Container App

Skill Hunter

Inline legal reference previews on sso.agc.gov.sg and www.elitigation.sg. Hover over a red underline while keeping the source page’s original layout.

Status unavailable

Enable Skill Hunter in Safari’s extension settings and allow access to SSO and eLitigation.

What ships in this extension

  • A toolbar popup with page properties and an inline preview toggle.
  • Red underlines and hover cards for supported legal references.
  • The source page’s layout, navigation, and controls stay in place.
  • No analytics or remote code. Reference lookups use the official source sites.

How to use it

  1. Enable the extension in Safari Settings and allow access to both supported sites.
  2. Open legislation on SSO or a judgment on eLitigation.
  3. Click the Skill Hunter toolbar icon and turn inline previews on.
  4. Hover over or focus a red underline to see its preview. Press Escape to dismiss it.
  5. Turn inline previews off in the toolbar popup to remove the added underlines.

Previews depend on readable official sources and the correct legislation version. A source may open in an inactive tab when its text needs to render in the browser.

Settings

The inline preview setting is saved locally in your browser. Once enabled, it applies to supported pages until you turn it off.

  • Site access is limited to SSO and eLitigation.
  • Reference previews fetch source text from those sites on demand.
  • Page text is not saved in browser storage or sent to an extension backend.
